=== COMPLIANCE ASSESSMENT ===
**Compliance Status:** Non-Compliant

**Violations Found:**
1. SCTP stream counts are set too low: SCTP_INSTREAMS = 2, SCTP_OUTSTREAMS = 2.
2. Reserved Tracking Area Code (TAC) values include 0x0000 and 0xfffe; here TAC=1 is used which is allowed, so no issue on TAC.
3. No explicit flaws in other fields per given config.

**Specification References:**
- O-RAN WG3 Interface SCTP Configuration Guidelines: SCTP stream count MUST be at least 10 input and 10 output streams for reliability and future scalability.
- 3GPP TS 38.401 (NG-RAN Architecture): SCTP streams minimum requirements for F1 interface depend on deployment; standard minimum is 10 streams.
- ETSI GR NFV-SEC 012: SCTP streams less than required expose the system to risk of stream exhaustion and possible DoS conditions.

**Recommended Code Modifications:**
- Increase `SCTP_INSTREAMS` and `SCTP_OUTSTREAMS` from 2 to 10.

**Security Impact Analysis:**
- Using only 2 SCTP streams for input/output can lead to early exhaustion of SCTP streams under high load or concurrent sessions, degrading the stability of the control/user plane communication and causing potential denial of service or interruptions.
- Increasing to 10 streams aligns with 3GPP and O-RAN recommended values to ensure sufficient buffer capacity against multiple concurrent connections and improves robustness.

**Out-of-Scope Recommendations:**
- Implement TLS or IPsec for underlying transport security.
- Enable SCTP heartbeat and restart timers.
- Use secure, non-default ports.
