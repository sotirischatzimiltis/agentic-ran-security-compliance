*   **3GPP TS 29.281 (GTP-U)**: Specifies the GPRS Tunnelling Protocol User Plane (GTP-U) for carrying user data in both 4G (e.g., S1-U) and 5G (N3) networks. While the port 2152 is standard for GTP-U, its specific assignment to "S1U" on a 5G SA CU-UP is the issue.

*   **Protocol Mismatch Attacks**: Attempts to communicate using S1-U specific protocols that are not intended for a 5G SA context could lead to unpredictable behavior, resource exhaustion, or denial of service if the software incorrectly handles such attempts.
*   **Misconfiguration Exploitation**: If the S1-U interface were to be inadvertently activated or remain partially open, it could lead to improper routing of traffic, bypass of security policies intended for 5G interfaces, or exposure to vulnerabilities present in legacy interface stacks.
