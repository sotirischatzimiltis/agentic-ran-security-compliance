2. drb_integrity = "no" disables integrity protection for DRB, a significant security risk violating 3GPP 5G NG-RAN security principles.

Fixing phase:

1. Change ciphering_algorithms to at least "nea1" (128-NEA1 - SNOW 3G), a valid secure ciphering algorithm per 3GPP TS 33.501 Annex D.
   Spec: 3GPP TS 33.501 states NEA0 is null ciphering (no protection), use at least NEA1 for secure configuration.

2. Set drb_integrity = "yes" to enable integrity protection on user data (DRB).
   Spec: Per 3GPP TS 33.501, integrity protection should be configured for DRBs.

3. Increase SCTP_INSTREAMS and SCTP_OUTSTREAMS from 2 to at least 10.
   Spec: SCTP streams fewer than 10 can affect performance and may be considered insecure due to limited multiplexing capacity.

4. Change local_s_portc and remote_s_portc from 501 and 500 to standard SCTP port 36412 used for F1 or NG interfaces.
   Spec: 3GPP TS 38.473/F1 and TS 38.413/NG specify SCTP port 36412 for gNB to CU/F1/AP interfaces.

5. Change local_s_address and remote_s_address to valid non-loopback IPs consistent with the other interface subnet IPs (e.g., 192.168.18.x).
   Spec: Use routable or management IPs to avoid loopback binding which is insecure and impractical in deployed networks.
