Violations Found:

1) Use of local_s_portc = 501 and remote_s_portc=500 may conflict with reserved ports for IPsec/IKE and could pose security risks. According to best practices, avoid using well-known ports reserved for other protocols.

Specification Reference:
3GPP TS 29.281 (GTP), 3GPP TS 33.501 (Security architecture), and IANA port assignments; Standard GTP ports are 2152 for user data, F1-C and F1-U usually use SCTP ports dynamically allocated or assigned in allowed ranges.

Risk:
Using reserved ports like 500 and 501 for SCTP or control signaling can cause conflicts, expose system to unwanted interference or attacks.

Recommended Fix:
Change local_s_portc and remote_s_portc to ports not reserved by other protocols; for F1-C SCTP, ports 36412 or others are used approximately. We will minimally change ports to 36412 local, 36413 remote as safer SCTP ports.

2) SD=0xffffff for SNSSAI slice description is not compliant as per 3GPP TS 23.003 standard which defines SD as a 3-octet (24-bit) slice differentiation. 0xffffff is maximum unsigned 24-bit value which may be reserved or invalid.

Specification Reference:
3GPP TS 23.003 clause on Slice Differentiator (SD) values must be within valid ranges and avoid reserved values.

Risk:
Incorrect SD values can lead to slice selection errors or security mismatches.

Recommended Fix:
Change SD field to 0x000000 (valid default) to represent unassigned slice differentiator.

3) SCTP INSTREAMS and OUTSTREAMS set to 2 — minimum SCTP streams required are at least 10 as per 3GPP F1 interface specification.

Specification Reference:
3GPP TS 38.413 requires SCTP streams to meet minimum counts for reliable transport.

Risk:
Low stream counts can limit communication reliability and affect session security.

Recommended Fix:
Increase SCTP_INSTREAMS and SCTP_OUTSTREAMS to 10 each.
