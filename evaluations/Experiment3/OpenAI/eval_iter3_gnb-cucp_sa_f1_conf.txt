- integrity_algorithms = ("nia2", "nia0"):
  "nia0" is null integrity protection from 3GPP TS 33.501 and provides no integrity protection. "nia2" is a valid integrity algorithm.

- drb_ciphering = "yes":
  This enables ciphering for DRB.

  This disables integrity protection for DRB. According to 3GPP and O-RAN standards, integrity protection SHOULD be enabled for DRBs to ensure data integrity and avoid tampering.
