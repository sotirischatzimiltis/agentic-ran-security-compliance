#### **1. Ciphering Algorithms Configuration**
- **Configuration Field**: `security.ciphering_algorithms = ( "nea0" )`
- **Violation**:
  - The use of `nea0` (null ciphering) is explicitly prohibited for **user plane (DRB)** and **control plane** protection in **3GPP TS 33.501 V18.9.0, Annex D** and **O-RAN WG11 Security Requirements**.
  - `nea0` generates a keystream of all zeroes, providing **no confidentiality protection**.
  - While `nea0` may be permitted for **emergency calls or specific testing scenarios**, it **must not** be the **only** or **preferred** algorithm in an operational CU configuration.
  - The configuration explicitly enables `drb_ciphering = "yes"`, which **requires** a non-null ciphering algorithm for DRBs.

- **Standard Reference**:
  - **3GPP TS 33.501 V18.9.0, Annex D.1**:
    > *"The NEA0 algorithm shall be implemented such that it generates a KEYSTREAM of all zeroes... The NEA0 algorithm shall not be used for ciphering of user plane data or control plane signalling in operational deployments."*
  - **Filename**: `ts_133501v180900p.pdf`

- **Security Risk**:
  - **No encryption** for user plane or control plane traffic, leading to **eavesdropping, data tampering, and session hijacking**.
  - Violates **3GPP and O-RAN security baselines** for confidentiality.

- **Fix Applied**:
  - Removed `"nea0"` from the `ciphering_algorithms` list.
  - Retained only **NEA1 (128-NEA1), NEA2 (128-NEA2), or NEA3 (128-AES-CTR)** as permitted by 3GPP.


  - **Filename**: `ts_133501v180900p.pdf`

- **Security Risk**:
