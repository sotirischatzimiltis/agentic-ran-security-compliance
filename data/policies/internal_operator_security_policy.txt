INTERNAL OPERATOR SECURITY POLICY
RAN SECURITY COMPLIANCE BASELINE
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-01-19
Scope: UE ↔ gNB (Access Stratum / PDCP / DRBs)
Applies to: Production deployments only

================================================================
POLICY OP-SEC-001
Title: Non-NULL Ciphering for User-Plane Traffic
----------------------------------------------------------------
Rule:
User-plane traffic between the CU and the UE SHALL use a non-NULL
ciphering algorithm.

Details:
The ciphering algorithm NEA0 (NULL ciphering) SHALL NOT be used
for production deployments. At least one of the following
algorithms MUST be configured:
- 128-NEA1
- 128-NEA2
- 128-NEA3

Rationale:
Prevent transmission of user data in clear text.

Derived from (informative):
3GPP TS 33.501 §5.3.2
("Confidentiality protection should be used whenever regulations permit")

Severity: Critical

================================================================
POLICY OP-SEC-002
Title: Disable NULL Integrity Algorithms
----------------------------------------------------------------
Rule:
NULL integrity algorithms SHALL NOT be enabled in production
deployments.

Details:
The integrity algorithm NIA0 (NULL integrity) SHALL be removed
from the allowed integrity algorithm list.

This policy assumes that unauthenticated emergency sessions
are not required in the deployment.

Rationale:
Prevent integrity downgrade attacks and ensure message integrity.

Derived from (informative):
3GPP TS 33.501 §5.3.3
("NIA0 shall be disabled in gNB deployments where unauthenticated
emergency sessions are not required")

Severity: Critical

================================================================
POLICY OP-SEC-003
Title: Enable User-Plane Integrity for DRBs
----------------------------------------------------------------
Rule:
Integrity protection for user-plane traffic SHALL be enabled.

Details:
Integrity protection MUST be enabled for all Data Radio Bearers
(DRBs) in production deployments.

Rationale:
Prevent tampering and replay attacks on user-plane traffic.

Derived from (informative):
3GPP TS 33.501 §5.3.3 (integrity protection support)
Operator security best practices

Severity: High
